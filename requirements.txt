# PATE on RT-IoT2022

Reproducible **Private Aggregation of Teacher Ensembles (PATE)** experiments for multi-class intrusion detection using the RT-IoT2022 dataset.

This repository evaluates **15 teacher–student architectural configurations** with the classic Laplace noisy-max mechanism and a full **moments accountant** (data-dependent + data-independent privacy analysis).
